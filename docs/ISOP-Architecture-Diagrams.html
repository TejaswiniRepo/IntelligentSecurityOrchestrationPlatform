All fraud signals in ISOP are simulated. The AI planner advises which checks to run; only the deterministic policy engine decides ALLOW, REVIEW or REJECT.

1. High-level architecture

A registration is investigated entirely on one laptop. The AI planner only advises which extra checks to run; the deterministic policy engine alone decides ALLOW, REVIEW or REJECT, and every step is recorded and streamed to the dashboard.
